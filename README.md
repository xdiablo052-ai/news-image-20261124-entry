A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) entry for **news-image-20261124**: one image that sums up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026.

The poster is a newspaper front page in a single frame, the date named in full at the top:

- **The Gulf** — a fourth tanker hits a mine in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran's Revolutionary Guards say they attacked a container ship and set terms to reopen the chokepoint
- **War** — Ukraine's naval drones sink the Russian corvette Merkury in Benghazi port and hit the cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland
- **Justice** — a US jury convicts Huawei of stealing trade secrets and bank fraud, acquitting it of racketeering
- **Climate** — COP31 in Antalya collapses with no deal, the first failed climate talks since Copenhagen 2009

Ticker: Houthi strike hits Riyadh tower · Senegal retakes AFCON crown · ICJ: Myanmar guilty of genocide · Nvidia reinstates Huang as CEO.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory, checking it against the gate's 1 MiB limit first. The image is 1024×1024 PNG, drawn as a poster and rendered locally.
